Privacy Policy — Get Clocked

Effective October 4, 2026

What we collect

Almost nothing. Get Clocked has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. The game never sends your data anywhere except one anonymous check described below.

What stays on your device

Everything the game remembers lives in your browser's local storage on your own device, and it never leaves:

  • Settings — sound, animations, theme, and whether spicy categories are on.
  • Player names you type in and your game setup — number of rounds, chosen categories.
  • Game history — your last 50 results, for the history screen.
  • Remove Ads entitlement — a flag that records you own the Remove Ads purchase on this device.

You can wipe all of it at any time with the "Reset all data" button in Settings.

The one network request

On launch, the app checks an ad feature-flag service for one true/false value ("are ads on?"). The request itself carries no personal information beyond standard connection details, like your IP address, which every website sees. All it asks is which app is checking. If ads are ever turned on, see "Ads" below.

Ads

Ads are currently switched off and none are shown. If enabled in the future, the app will show either ads through the app store's own SDK or simple cross-promo cards for our own other games — both without tracking you across apps or sites.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, email, or payment details. The app only stores the yes/no result of the purchase on your device so ads stay off.

Children

Get Clocked is a general-audience party game and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
